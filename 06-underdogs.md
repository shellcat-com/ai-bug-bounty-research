## Why Underdogs Matter for Your Article

1. **They share more.** Big accounts hoard their competitive advantage. Underdogs document their journey publicly.

2. **They're the adoption signal.** When 30-follower accounts are building AI bug bounty workflows, it's real. Not hype.

3. **They're the next wave.** The @mdp_sec of 2027 is currently an underdog with 200 followers and a GitHub repo nobody has seen yet.

4. **They make your article different.** Everyone cites @Rhynorater and @rez0__. Nobody is profiling @horizonfps or @elkrispis. You found them first.
